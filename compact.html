Your proposed match at a glance

Name and affiliation stay hidden until you have both signed.

This document details the terms of engagement set by the ISEV/SNEV Mentorship Program Committee. The checklist should be revisited by the mentor and mentee together during an initial virtual meeting (~1 hour) to confirm expectations and goals align.

1. Mentoring goals and objectives

  • Mentor: provide guidance on overarching career development - career transitions, professional networking, job search strategies, communication and leadership, long-term career trajectories in the EV field - avoiding advice on the specifics of current research projects.
  • Mentee: identify personal and professional development areas outside immediate research tasks, aligned with your broader career aspirations.
  • Agree on short- and long-term milestones tailored to these career-focused goals.

2. Communication guidelines

  • Establish a mutually agreed meeting frequency (the program expectation is roughly bi-monthly - every other month - for one year) and preferred channels.
  • Set clear expectations for response times and scheduling flexibility.
  • Define a procedure for rescheduling missed sessions.

3. Roles and responsibilities

  • Mentor: provide guidance based on experience in the EV community; share networking opportunities and best practices.
  • Mentee: prepare for meetings with specific questions; actively set the agenda for each meeting.
  • Success depends on both parties' active participation.

4. Confidentiality and respect

  • Treat all shared information, research ideas, and personal insights as confidential.
  • Communicate respectfully and constructively in a non-discriminatory, inclusive, supportive environment.

5. Feedback, evaluation, and adaptation

  • Exchange honest, constructive feedback routinely; schedule periodic check-ins against goals; consider keeping session notes.

6. Ethics and professional standards

  • Follow the ethical guidelines and code of conduct of the International Society for Extracellular Vesicles (ISEV Bylaws).
  • Disclose any potential conflicts of interest that might affect the relationship.

7. Dispute resolution and support

  • Issues are addressed first between the pair, with mediation from the program committee if needed.
  • Either party may end the relationship with prior discussion to allow a smooth transition.

8. Commitment & sign-off

  • Both mentor and mentee confirm they have read, understood, and agree to abide by these terms.
  • Participation is voluntary, with a shared commitment to making the relationship as enriching as possible.
