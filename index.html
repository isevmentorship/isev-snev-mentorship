Professional-development mentorship for EV researchers

The ISEV-SNEV Mentorship Program pairs extracellular vesicle researchers one-to-one for a 12-month professional-development relationship - matched on career topics, EV focus areas, and time zone, with identities kept private until both sides agree to proceed.

This is a career-development program, not technical research consulting. Pairs focus on things like grant-writing, career transitions, leadership, communication, and navigating the EV community - not on troubleshooting a specific assay.

Apply

About the program

Applications are reviewed by a committee drawn from ISEV and SNEV. Once accepted, each mentee is paired with a single mentor based on ranked career topics, EV focus areas, and time zone. Names and institutions stay hidden while both sides confirm the match by signing a short program compact; identities are revealed once both have signed, and the 12-month relationship begins.

How it works

Apply

Tell us your role (mentor or mentee), career stage, EV focus area, ranked career topics, time zone, and availability. Takes about 5 minutes.

Get matched

Our matcher pairs you with your single best-fit counterpart, scored on your ranked career topics, EV focus areas, and time zone.

Sign the compact

You see a blinded summary of your match and accept by signing the Mentorship Program Compact online. Once both of you have signed, identities are revealed.

Meet for 12 months

Pairs meet bi-monthly for a year, with a mid-year check-in from the committee. A closeout survey informs the next cycle.

For mentees

  • Graduate students, postdocs, and early-career researchers working in, or moving into, EV biology who want structured career development over a year.
  • Clinician-scientists and industry scientists looking for an external perspective on a career transition.
  • Mentees should bring a concrete set of career topics they want to work on - not a specific experiment they want debugged.

ISEV membership is required to apply.

For mentors

  • PIs, senior postdocs, staff scientists, and senior industry researchers willing to meet with each mentee bi-monthly (every other month) for 12 months - roughly one hour per meeting.
  • Mentors choose to support 1-3 mentees per cycle, jointly or separately.
  • Mentors from underrepresented regions and backgrounds are especially encouraged to apply.
  • Prior mentoring experience is not required - we provide a mentor toolkit during onboarding.

Active ISEV membership is required for mentors.

Apply

Applications open twice a year: October 1-15 and April 15 - May 15. The form takes about 5 minutes and the committee aims to respond within two weeks of the window closing.

Open the application form

You can apply as a mentor or mentee on the same form - just pick your role at the top.

Frequently asked questions

Why keep things blinded at first?
Blinding reduces the influence of prestige, nationality, and prior acquaintance on initial match selection. It keeps the focus on training goals and working-style fit. Once both parties mutually choose, identities are revealed before any meeting.
What happens if I don't find a match I like?
You'll see a blinded summary of your proposed match when you're asked to sign the compact. If it doesn't feel right, reply to the committee email instead of signing - we'll return you to the pool for the next match run. If your match doesn't confirm within two weeks, you go back into the pool automatically.
What's the expected time commitment?
A typical pairing meets bi-monthly (every other month) for twelve months - roughly one hour per meeting, plus light asynchronous communication. That's six meetings over the year. Pairs are free to continue informally afterwards.
Is this program for technical research advice?
No. This is a professional-development program. Pairs focus on career topics - grant-writing, transitions, leadership, communication, networking, publishing strategy, and so on. We ask mentors and mentees to steer away from discussing the specifics of the mentee's current research project, both to protect intellectual property and to keep the relationship focused on career growth rather than ad-hoc consulting.
Is there a cost?
No. Participation is free for both mentors and mentees, thanks to ISEV and SNEV support.
How many mentees can a mentor support?
Mentors choose their own support level when applying: 1, 2, or 3 mentees per 12-month cycle. Mentors supporting multiple mentees may be paired at different times as application windows run.
Who runs the program?
The program is operated jointly by the International Society for Extracellular Vesicles (ISEV) and the Student Network for EVs (SNEV), with a volunteer matching committee drawn from both organizations.
How do I reach the committee with a question?
Email isevmentorship@gmail.com. We try to answer within a few working days.
